### W3 — Repair tip: "Weber won't light?" (Mon 6/15) · asset: `weber-tip-ignite.png`
**FB:** 🔧 **Weber won't light? Before you panic, check these 3 things:**
1. **Igniter battery** — that AA in the ignition button dies more often than the igniter itself.
2. **Burner tubes** — spiders love to nest in them; a clogged tube won't carry flame across.
3. **Regulator** — if you get a weak flame after hooking up propane, it may be in "bypass." Turn everything off, disconnect, wait 30 sec, reconnect slowly.
Still nothing? That's what we're here for — we'll diagnose and fix it on the spot. 👉 /quote
