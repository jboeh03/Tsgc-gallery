POSTING NOTES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

IMAGE TO PAIR
- Best: a strong after photo of the restored Weber Spirit II — grates gleaming,
  hood open, on a clean patio. This is the money shot. One great photo beats a
  composite here.
- Alternative: side-by-side before/after composite using generate-fb-composites.py.
  Shows the transformation and earns the click.
- Do NOT pair with a generic stock photo. The grill Jeff personally restored is
  the story — show it.

LEGAL / PLATFORM COMPLIANCE
- Do not require users to tag someone as a condition of entry. The post copy
  above correctly frames it as encouragement, not a requirement.
- Facebook's promotion policies prohibit requiring tags to enter. The official
  entry method is the on-site form at /giveaway — no tag required.
- If running as a Facebook Ads boosted post: Facebook may restrict sweepstakes
  promotion in ad content. Review current ad policies before boosting.
- Include "No purchase necessary" language either in the post body or in the
  first comment. It appears in the Official Rules but visible placement is
  good practice.

FB ALGORITHM TIPS
- External links get throttled in reach. Workaround: post the body natively,
  drop the link (tristategrillcleaning.com/giveaway) in the FIRST COMMENT
  immediately after publishing. The audience still sees it; FB's algorithm
  doesn't penalize the reach as heavily.
- Pin this post to the top of the page through the giveaway close date.
- Engagement drives reach. Responding to every comment (especially veteran
  tags) in the first 2 hours dramatically increases organic distribution.

CROSS-POSTING
- Instagram: use the SHORT VERSION. Drop the URL in bio + first comment
  (IG doesn't make caption links clickable). Add a story with a "Link in bio"
  swipe-up or sticker pointing to /giveaway.
- Nextdoor: SHORT VERSION works well. Post in your neighborhood and adjacent
  ones in your service area. The "tag a veteran" angle plays especially well
  here — Nextdoor skews toward established homeowners with grills.
- Facebook Groups (local Cincinnati / NKY neighborhood groups): Check group
  rules for giveaway posts. Many local groups allow them with mod approval.

TIMING
- Open day post: publish on the day the giveaway opens (Friday, June 26).
  Best window: 9–11 AM on a weekday for local-biz audience.
- Mid-run reminder: 3–4 days before close. Shorter copy: "Last few days to
  enter — link in comments."
- Close-day post: "Entries close tonight at 11:59 PM. One more chance."
  Post the morning of Saturday, July 4.
- Winner announcement: Tuesday, July 7. Announce publicly on the page
  (with winner's consent) for social proof and engagement.
