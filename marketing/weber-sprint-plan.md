## Core message

**Webers are built to last 15+ years if you take care of them. This sprint gets
local Webers cooking like new before peak summer — at the best price I'll run all year.**

That's it. Everything else is an angle on that one idea.

### Angles to rotate (don't lean on just one)

1. **Built to last.** A Weber is worth keeping. A deep clean adds years.
   Don't replace a $900 grill over baked-on carbon.
2. **The real deep clean.** Not a wire brush over the grates — the cookbox,
   flavorizer bars, and hood stripped down. (tsg-016 cookbox shot is the proof.)
3. **Get it done before peak summer.** July 4th, the cookouts, the company —
   you want it cooking like new *before* the season hits, not during.
4. **Local + neighborly.** Veteran-founded, I drive these streets. Book with a
   neighbor and we both save you a trip — and knock 30% off.
5. **The ticker.** A real, public goal (50 Webers). People like being part of a
   thing that's happening on their street.

### Voice reminders

First person. Warm, direct, honest. Name the grill, the model, the neighborhood.
One soft CTA. No ALL CAPS, no emoji spray, no fake countdown-timer pressure.
The ticker *is* the urgency — I don't need to manufacture more.

## 3 concrete ways to drive the 30% "book with a neighbor" tier

1. **"Tag your grill buddy" callout.**
   On the bundle-push posts, end with: *"Know a neighbor with a Weber? Tag them — if you
   both book, you both get 30% off and I'll knock out both in one trip."* Turns the post
   into a referral engine; the tag does the recruiting for me. Works best on FB + Nextdoor
   where people actually know each other.

2. **Auto-detect the bundle on /weber.**
   When two bookings land on the same street / ZIP within the sprint, the page (or I,
   manually) flags both for the 30% rate even if they booked separately. Then I text both:
   *"Looks like your neighbor booked too — I bumped you both to 30%."* Surprise upgrade =
   a story they'll share, and it removes the "I have to coordinate with someone" friction.

3. **"First Weber on the block, second one's 30%" street offer.**
   When I finish a job, before I leave I tell the customer: *"If a neighbor books their
   Weber this week off the back of yours, you both drop to 30%."* Then a quick Nextdoor /
   group post tagged to that exact street: *"Just deep-cleaned a Weber on [street] — any
   neighbor who books this week, you both get 30%."* Uses the truck-in-the-driveway
   social proof while it's hot.
